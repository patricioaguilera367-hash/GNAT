- Incremental JSONL summaries/schemas and explicit correlation markers; no models for log summarization. Distinguish observed bodies, loaded-client evidence and inference.
- Use known codes only; geometry tests use a few known IDs. Batch tests maximum two known IDs; never search maximum server limits by load. No area enumeration without a verified read contract and bounded user-authorized area.
- Strict TDD: enabled by supplied AGENTS.md; native runner source confirmed by tests/diagnostic.test.js. New runner: `node --test gnat-research/tests/*.test.cjs` once created. Require observed RED, GREEN and final checks.
