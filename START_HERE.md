## Source-of-truth rules

- Original source bytes/hashes establish what code exists, not correctness in every case.
- Current ledger supersedes old search/selection report gaps only for covered samples. Preserve historical reports rather than flattening them into universal current facts.
- Recovered sector findings supplement, not silently rewrite, the ledger. They are not a machine-readable electrical graph.
- Transformed observed fixtures preserve selected contracts, not raw bodies. Synthetic fixtures are invented. Neither is authoritative inventory.
- Implementation/geolocation/model documents are proposals. Source/tests determine implemented behavior.
- Empty cached tabs, dropped fields, omitted payloads and failed collection are not negative business evidence.

## Context that must survive

S/E means substation. Transformer **CD of S/E** differs from pole **COD**; do not invent an expansion of CD. Physical hosting, UI hierarchy and electrical connectivity are distinct relations. Codes can return several asset kinds/IDs: preserve ambiguity and identity roles. A line, shared label, crossing or nearby coordinate is not proof of electrical connection.

There is enough evidence to design consultation/maps/fiches with honest uncertainty. There is NOT enough for rigorous sector/isolation conclusions or maneuver guidance. Read permission, retention rights, authentication, graph relations and state quality/freshness remain feature-specific gates.
